# Limitations

- The original dataset acquisition event is not preserved in Git history; Kaggle attribution is probable, not proven.
- Source review percentages are not clinical efficacy measures.
- Retail prices, costs, profits, sales quantities, expiry dates, competitor prices and movement categories are simulated.
- The simulation does not model taxes, discounts, batch-level expiry, supplier lead time, prescriptions, seasonality, regional price variation or regulatory price controls.
- `trust_score` is not a validated medical metric.
- The dashboard must not be used for patient treatment, dispensing, purchasing or commercial price decisions.
- No claim of actual waste reduction, revenue increase or profit improvement is made.
